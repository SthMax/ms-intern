## Verified disclosure

The AMAC-hosted Qianxun (千询) case describes an AI/large-model fixed-income
workflow spanning quotations, counterpart matching, chat-to-instruction parsing,
and bond fundamentals. The Xingbao (兴宝) assistant and Qtrade integration are
explicit. LRU caching, Oracle Data Guard and Kafka are disclosed platform
components; no foundation-model name or model-supplier contract is specified.

The workflow includes human trader follow-up and manager instruction submission
(PDF pp. 4–5). It should not be described as entirely autonomous trading.
The case reports approximately three hours saved per trader per day and over
50% growth in inquiry conversations (PDF p. 6); measurement details and causal
attribution are limited. Stage: operational use reported.
