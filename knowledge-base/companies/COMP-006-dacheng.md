# Dacheng / 大成基金管理有限公司

## Verified disclosure

The AMAC-hosted fixed-income platform case explicitly names NLP and **千问 2.5**
for intelligent trading (PDF p. 9, printed p. 8). It describes transaction-element
semantic recognition within a wider platform, alongside Flink, SpringCloud,
Vue2.0, RPA and O32 integration (PDF pp. 4, 6, 11).

These are component/integration disclosures. They do not identify an external
model-implementation contract. Stage: operational platform reported.

The case reports adoption across ten first-level and fourteen second-level
business units. Its fixed-income AUM-growth discussion is a business outcome,
not proof of causal AI ROI. The code/LLM components should not be credited with
all platform benefits.
