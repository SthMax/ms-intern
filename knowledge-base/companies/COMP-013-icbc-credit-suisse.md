# ICBC Credit Suisse / 工银瑞信基金管理有限公司

## Research conclusion

The fund manager's original AMAC-hosted pension-platform case documents
**FundGPT**, a privately deployed group of language, multimodal, speech and
embedding models, with concrete document and research applications. AMAC's
index lists the case on **21 January 2026** (FUND-313). The case states FundGPT
was publicly launched at the 2023 services trade fair (FUND-312, PDF p. 11).

These disclosures name the fund manager's systems. They do not attribute
the bank parent's broader AI estate to its fund subsidiary.

LLM trading authority or final document-approval policy is established.

## Reported outcomes and follow-up evidence

The case reports more than **110,000** bid/report documents produced through the
wider operating workflow (PDF p. 9). It also reports **500+ person-months saved
annually** and **RMB 45 million cumulative savings** over its multi-year platform
effort (PDF p. 10). These encompass systems integration, RPA and other methods;
they are **not isolated LLM ROI**, and no independent cost audit is supplied.

A May 2026 narrative interview/report with CIO Wang Jian reports DeepSeek and Qwen as
base-model choices, FundGPT model switching through a protocol bridge, and
applications in bids, document checks, CRM transcription and coding (FUND-314,
sections III and V). It is reporter-mediated narrative, not a first-person
transcript; retain these additional technical details as candidate evidence until
confirmed by an original company/technical source. The page's legal
characterisations are not used in this company note.

A separate Wang Jian article reproduced from 中国金融电脑 reports over five
million LLM calls in 2025, monthly peak 900,000 (FUND-315, section I.2). The
original publication has not been opened, so retain this specific volume figure
as **secondary/reprint pending original**, outside primary-only headline counts.

## Technology partners and asset size

Hundsun's original **2023 sustainable-development report**, downloaded from
CNINFO, names **Hundsun Juyuan, ICBC Credit Suisse, ICBC Technology and Zhipu AI**
as FundGPT collaborators, under guidance from ICBC's group technology department
(FUND-316, PDF p. 49 / printed p. 38). It specifically assigns Juyuan financial
training-corpus provision, financial-model fine-tuning and research-technology
support. The exact division of delivery work among the other partners and
contract values remain undisclosed. This is primary vendor evidence of the
collaboration, not a standalone validation of customer ROI.

The AMAC case reports **total company AUM above RMB 2 trillion at 2024-12-31**
(PDF p. 10). This historical broad measure is not same-date public-fund AUM.
No current official public-fund AUM is established here; use the separate
size-first universe with its scope intact.
