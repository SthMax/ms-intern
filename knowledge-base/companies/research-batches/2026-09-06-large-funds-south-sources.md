## Retrieval notes

- Search discovery used both built-in web and Exa, followed by original links.
  Exa aliases are not independent evidence providers.
- Southern's old `P020260123599025511261.pdf` returned 404. AMAC's live index
  exposed a working replacement and two text-identical alternatives. Current
  inspected wording is 小喃同学. A duplicate is not a second initiative.
- Text extraction missed China Universal's R1/QianWen labels in Figure 2. Visual
  review supplied the exact diagram evidence, rather than inferring models from
  generic news coverage.
- Direct official HTML avoided two stale-extraction traps: Southern's AI terms
  were absent from Exa's copy, and Bosera's AUM date lagged the live profile.
- For FUND-225, the official Chinese PDF had broken CNS1 font/encoding output;
  the corresponding official English document's complete relevant pages were
  rendered and checked. No missing Chinese legal text was reconstructed.
- The 99fund news search endpoint was read from its public page/script and used
  only to retrieve public article listings. Its DeepSeek result links to media;
  no sign-in, account data or external write occurred.
