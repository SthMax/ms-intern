## Verifiable leads and source provenance

The 27 November 2025 南方+ report describes **基于AI大模型的投研要素表智能助手**
as used by researchers to populate structured research forms (FUND-354).
It describes finance-specific fine-tuning, external/internal API data integration,
semantic extraction, and agents splitting collection/analysis/synthesis tasks.
The report's award and operating claims await an original company/award-case
source. Its references to reasoning chains do not establish validated
interpretability or faithful explanations.

The 1 June 2026 Sina article credits **中欧基金** and links to its WeChat account
(FUND-355). Opening that account's article reveals **“内容转载自：中国基金报”**
(FUND-356). Thus the chain is media report → company-account reprint → Sina,
not three independent primary confirmations.

That report attributes to China Europe, as of May 2026, a research agent with
100+ Skills, approximately 60% improved information-retrieval efficiency and
approximately 50% improved research-form preparation efficiency. These remain
**secondary, company-attributed numbers** with missing measurement protocols.
The adjacent Shanghai Jiao Tong optimisation collaboration concerns portfolio
optimisation; do not reclassify its speedup as an LLM result.
