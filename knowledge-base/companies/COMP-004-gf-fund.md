## Verified disclosure

GF's official service agreement describes an intelligent financial assistant
using DeepSeek and Qwen-family LLMs for generated responses, smart QA and
retrieval augmentation (PDF p. 1, clause I.1). Clause II.1 includes fund and
account-data queries, market information, holdings diagnosis, fund explanations,
and customer questions. The agreement distinguishes the assistant from formal
investment-advisory services.

Clause X.4 credits DeepSeek and Alibaba Cloud/Qwen as model origins
(PDF pp. 15–16). Open-model attribution does not demonstrate a paid partnership,
a specific running model version, or remote API consumption.

Stage: service documented in official terms; live availability and deployment
topology not tested. No launch date is printed in the reviewed document.

## Asset size

The official profile reports total managed assets above RMB 2 trillion at
31 December 2025 and 453 public funds. The retrieved total is not specifically
public-fund AUM; the product count is not an asset-size measure.

## Limits

No operational ROI or hosting conclusion is supported. The PDF's printed footer
repeats “6”; use the physical PDF page index for retrieval. Human review pending.
