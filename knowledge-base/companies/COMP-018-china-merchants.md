## Specific leads worth verifying

The 5 March 2025 original 21世纪经济报道 survey names 招商基金 among managers
reported to have privately deployed DeepSeek (FUND-350, 拥抱AI). This is a
journalist's survey, not an opened company announcement.

A fuller 11 November 2025 article, reproduced by Tonghuashun from 财经网 and
credited to **许楠楠**, describes (FUND-351, AI模型矩阵初步发展成形):

- An AI portal with **DeepSeek R1 and QWQ-32b**, an assistant system and a
  workflow/model-orchestration platform.
- Research knowledge integration, summaries, meeting transcription/minutes
  and research translation.
- Customer support integrating shareholder **China Merchants Bank's 小招X**
  engine and knowledge base; chat-to-ticket summaries.
- TA/FA knowledge assistants and a wider AI/OCR operating workflow said to
  process 40,000+ materials monthly. The adjacent 350+ RPA scenarios across
  18 departments are **RPA measures**, not LLM outcomes.
- Legal/internal-policy knowledge Q&A and related review tools.

These are promising, manually traceable **reported claims**, not confirmed
primary facts. The source distinguishes current applications from planned
dynamic asset-allocation functions; do not turn the latter into deployment.
An assertion of zero errors is not a validated error-rate evaluation.

## Entity, architecture and partner limits

This note concerns 招商基金. **招商资管、招商证券 and 招商银行 are different
entities.** The reported 小招X integration is a narrowly described fund-manager
use of a parent's capability; the parent's full AI application counts cannot be
assigned to the fund manager. Foundation-model origin, integration endpoint and
paid implementation partner remain different fields.

GPU models/counts, private deployment boundaries, access controls, human
approval steps and independently measured LLM ROI are not established by this
pass. Comparable AUM comes from the separate size screen; no new company-origin
AUM is asserted here.
