## What this changes in the presentation

The evidence supports a more precise narrative than “large funds are exploring
DeepSeek.” Several managers describe shared model/data platforms with specific
research, engineering, document, trading-support and review workflows.

A useful comparison should show the **workflow around the model**: source
documents, retrieval or extraction, data/tool connections, human decisions,
and recorded outcomes. That is research interpretation of the source set, not
a recommendation to copy a peer system into COD.

There are now direct review/compliance examples at Penghua, China Universal and
Hua An. Their documents support assistance, source-based checks and review
processes, rather than an inference that LLMs provide final legal sign-off.

## Comparability and uncertainty

- The size screen is consistent within one published table. Official firm/
  parent-filing AUM is separate and may include money funds, feeders, pensions,
  advisory activity or subsidiaries. Do not mix those numbers into a new rank.
- Reported task duration, user reach, suggested-edit adoption, detection rate and
  whole-platform cost measures are different metrics. No pooled peer ROI is
  calculated.
- Private, local, API and hybrid routes coexist in the evidence. A component
  name, supplier logo or private model does not settle all data flows.
- Named public users/partners are attributed to the manager in scope, not its
  bank/broker/insurance parent.
- The five unresolved managers remain in the coverage table. A media-heavy record
  is an evidence gap, not evidence of low adoption.
