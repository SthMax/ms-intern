## Research disposition

This is a **large-manager coverage gap with credible, detailed media leads**.
The focused follow-up did not locate an original company deployment announcement,
technical case, original vendor implementation record or issuer filing that
verifies the reported LLM deployment. It therefore adds **no verified adoption
initiative** to the main database. This describes our evidence position, not an
assertion that the manager does not use AI.

The official profile, **FUND-252**, identifies the onshore manager founded on
12 June 2003 and the shareholding split of Invesco and Great Wall Securities at
49% each. It expressly distinguishes fund-management operations from shareholder
operations. Invesco's global AI systems or global AUM cannot substitute for
evidence about this company.

## Verified asset-size evidence

**FUND-253**, Great Wall Securities' original 2025 annual report filed with SZSE,
states that 景顺长城基金's managed scale reached **RMB 926.4 billion
(9,264亿元)** and its non-money-fund ranking returned to the top ten.
The relevant sentence does not define whether the 9,264亿元 includes private
mandates, nor does it label that value as non-money AUM. Record it as **reported
manager AUM, scope not fully specified, FY2025**, rather than converting it into
a public-only/ex-money metric. The separate screening list supplies the
comparable 2026 Q2 measure.

The annual-report page also discusses AI tools in Great Wall Securities'
proprietary investment business. That is a different entity/business section;
it is not evidence of Invesco Great Wall's LLM adoption.
