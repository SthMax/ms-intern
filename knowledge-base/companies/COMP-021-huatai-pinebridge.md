## Specific 2026 lead

The 3 September 2026 article **华泰柏瑞量化团队：“人机协同”迈入深水区，AI投研构筑长期护城河**
is now archived from the 中国证券报 WeChat publication in a user-supplied
browser print PDF (FUND-359, pp. 1–3), alongside the Sina reprint (FUND-358).
It is a narrative report using statements attributed to 盛豪, not a
company-authored technical case or a first-person transcript.

The report describes current use of large models to structure announcements,
research notes and market-sentiment text; coding/data-organisation assistance;
and combining text signals with quantitative information for portfolio diagnosis
and event/risk monitoring. Hard constraints such as concentration and liquidity
are reportedly implemented as system rules, while staff decide how to act on
AI-assisted subjective-risk alerts.

Its later section on an assistant independently retrieving data, running code,
backtesting and iterating describes a **future direction**. Do not record that
future agent as already operational. PDF p. 3 expressly introduces this as a future vision. No base-model family, parameter count,
hosting arrangement, implementation vendor, GPU requirement or numerical
productivity result is disclosed.
