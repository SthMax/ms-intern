# 中华人民共和国标准化法（2017年修订）

[Official source](https://www.cnipa.gov.cn/art/2019/7/29/art_104_67809.html).

Relevant location: Articles 2, 18, 45.

## Verified source content

Article 2 distinguishes standard categories and mandatory national standards.
Article 18 describes group standards as adopted by agreement among group members
or voluntarily by society under the group's arrangements. Article 45 supplies
commencement of the revised law.

## Project interpretation

Do not label AMAC's group standard as universally mandatory legislation, or as
necessarily optional for every member in every context. Verify relevant member
agreements, association rules, regulatory incorporation, and internal adoption.
This note classifies the source; it does not determine MSIM's obligations.
