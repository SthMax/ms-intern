# 网络数据安全管理条例

[Official source](https://www.cac.gov.cn/2024-09/30/c_1729384452307680.htm).

Relevant location: Articles 2, 9, 12, 19, 31, 37, 64.

## Verified source content

Article 2 defines geographic scope. Article 9 addresses organizational and
technical safeguards and processor responsibility. Article 12 requires contracts,
oversight, and at least three years of records when providing or entrusting
personal information/important data. Article 19 addresses training-data security
for providers of generative-AI services. Articles 31 and 37 address important-data
risk assessment and cross-border treatment; Article 64 sets commencement.

## Project interpretation

Map prompts, retrieval corpora, outputs, logs, and model-training data separately.
Determine personal/important-data classification and roles before selecting the
applicable provision. The term “important data” should not be assigned merely
because a desk considers material commercially sensitive.
