# 证券期货业网络和信息安全管理办法

[Official source](https://www.csrc.gov.cn/csrc/c101953/c7202800/7202800/files/%E9%99%84%E4%BB%B61%EF%BC%9A%E8%AF%81%E5%88%B8%E6%9C%9F%E8%B4%A7%E4%B8%9A%E7%BD%91%E7%BB%9C%E5%92%8C%E4%BF%A1%E6%81%AF%E5%AE%89%E5%85%A8%E7%AE%A1%E7%90%86%E5%8A%9E%E6%B3%95.pdf).

Relevant location: Articles 2, 4, 29–34, 71, 75; PDF pp. 1–2, 9–11, 20–22.

[Official publication page](https://www.csrc.gov.cn/csrc/c101953/c7202800/content.shtml).

## Verified source content

Articles 2 and 4 address sector network/information-system scope and responsibility
that is not transferred or reduced by use of external services. Articles 29–34
address investor personal-information governance, necessity, lifecycle protection,
third-party provision and safeguards outside the institution's protection boundary.
Article 75 sets commencement and repeals the earlier Order 82.

## Project interpretation

The relevant boundary includes actual application, network, vendor, and data
flows. An internal server can still have external support, logs, or integrations.
Assess those flows before claiming the proposed system stays inside the relevant
security boundary.
