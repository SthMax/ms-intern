# 中华人民共和国个人信息保护法

[Official source](https://www.cac.gov.cn/2021-08/20/c_1631050028355286.htm).

Relevant location: Articles 13, 21, 24, 28, 55–56, 74.

## Verified source content

Article 13 lists several lawful processing bases; consent is not the only basis.
Article 21 requires agreed processor terms and oversight, limits processing to
the mandate, and restricts onward entrustment. Article 24 concerns automated
decisions using personal information: transparency/fairness, marketing choices,
and explanation/refusal rights for significantly consequential solely automated
decisions. Article 28 includes financial-account information among sensitive
information. Articles 55–56 require prior impact assessment for specified
activities and retention of assessment/processing records for at least three
years. Article 74 supplies commencement.

## Project interpretation

Identify whether the input/output contains personal information and what
processing activity occurs. Article 24 is not a blanket technical explainability
rule for every document summarizer. Public availability of an input does not
alone resolve its permissible processing basis.
