# 生成式人工智能服务管理暂行办法

[Official Chinese text](https://www.cac.gov.cn/2023-07/13/c_1690898327029107.htm).
Joint departmental measures, Order 15; signed 10 July and published 13 July 2023.
The promulgation paragraph specifies commencement on 15 August 2023.

## Verified source content

- Article 2 covers generative-AI services supplied to the public in mainland
  China. Its third paragraph excludes organizational research/application that
  does not supply such services to that public.
- Article 4(5) addresses transparency, accuracy, and reliability within the
  measures' scope.

## Project interpretation

Service audience is an applicability question distinct from hosting location.
An internal on-premise research assistant may meet the Article 2 exclusion, but
that depends on its actual users and service boundary. This exclusion does not
establish exemption from other laws or internal requirements. Do not transfer
Article 4(5) into an unconditional rule for every internal LLM.
