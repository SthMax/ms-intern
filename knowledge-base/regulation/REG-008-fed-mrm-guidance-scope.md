# Supervisory Guidance on Model Risk Management — April 2026

[Official guidance](https://www.federalreserve.gov/frrs/guidance/supervisory-guidance-on-model-risk-management.htm).

## Verified source content

- Section I describes guidance rather than enforceable prescriptive standards;
  footnote 1 preserves potential action for violations of law or unsafe practices.
- Section II and footnote 3 define scope. Generative and agentic AI are expressly
  outside it; institutional governance should determine controls for uncovered
  tools. Traditional quantitative and non-generative/non-agentic AI models remain
  addressed.
- Sections III–VII discuss proportionate review, validation/monitoring,
  governance, documentation, and vendor-model understanding.
