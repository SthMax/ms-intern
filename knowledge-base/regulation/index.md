## Phase 1 synthesis structure

For each of the three required themes, write:

1. Source-backed requirement/guidance, identifying legal force and status.
2. Relevant article/section/page and direct source link.
3. Applicability to the contemplated MSIM use, with uncertainty stated.
4. Research interpretation and implications for Phase 2.
5. Unresolved internal-policy or subject-matter questions.
