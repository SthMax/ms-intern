## Legal-research QA checklist

- Official source and exact provision captured.
- Publication, effective, amendment, and current-status dates checked.
- Applicability to this entity, service, user population, and deployment pattern
  treated as a question unless confirmed.
- Source wording separated from interpretation.
- Chinese original reviewed when controlling.
- Authorized legal/compliance review requested before a recommendation is called
  compliant.
