## Reading this register

- “verified-primary” applies to the narrow cited source content. It does not
  mean independent validation of a company's ROI, current legal clearance, or
  completed human review.
- Original AMAC project case reports are primary evidence that the named case
  was publicly documented. Company performance figures remain reported claims.
- A news report reposted on an official website remains a news report. FUND-006
  and FUND-090–093 are excluded from primary-only company/initiative counts.
- Publication dates missing from PDFs remain missing; dates in URL paths,
  filenames, and file metadata are not substituted.
- Several official profile pages are updated without changing their original
  page dates. AUM as-of date is recorded separately from publication/access.
- Each note contains the narrow claim, interpretation, locator, and limitations.
