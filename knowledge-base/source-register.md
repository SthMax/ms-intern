## Source-quality hierarchy

1. Official law/regulator text, formal filing, company announcement/report, or
   original technical documentation.
2. Official speech/Q&A, company case study, or vendor account.
3. Reputable independent reporting or research.
4. Search snippets, reposts, social media, and unattributed summaries for discovery.

Source quality is claim-specific. A company may establish that it announced a
project; a vendor may establish that it reported a customer case. Neither alone
necessarily verifies a production deployment or measured ROI. Record instrument
type and legal force separately from publisher authority.
