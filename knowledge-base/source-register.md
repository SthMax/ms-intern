## Source-quality hierarchy

1. Official law/regulator text, formal filing, company announcement/report, or
   original technical documentation.
2. Official speech, regulator Q&A, company/vendor case study.
3. Reputable independent reporting or research.
4. Search snippets, reposts, social media, and unattributed summaries—discovery
   only.
