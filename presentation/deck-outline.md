# Week 1–2 Checkpoint Presentation — 11 September 2026

This deck covers the two Week 1–2 targets only. The broader feasibility study,
pilot selection, benchmarking, infrastructure sizing, and TCO analysis belong to
the remaining internship roadmap.

Target: approximately 9–11 core slides plus an evidence appendix. Final length
should be adjusted after the audience and speaking time are confirmed. Each
factual slide must carry source IDs and manually verifiable links/footnotes.

1. **Internship context and objective hierarchy** — two-month objectives versus
   this Week 1–2 checkpoint.
2. **Checkpoint questions and research method** — inclusion rules, source
   hierarchy, evidence states, and limitations.
3. **Peer universe and database design** — company selection, asset-size measure,
   and the still-unconfirmed minimum count.
4. **Onshore fund-manager landscape** — verified initiatives by company and
   maturity.
5. **Use cases by function** — research, trading, risk, marketing, compliance,
   operations, with no inference beyond disclosures.
6. **Disclosed technology stacks and partners** — named models/vendors/platforms;
   distinguish fund statements from vendor claims.
7. **Reported outcomes and evidence quality** — what has actually been disclosed,
   plus material unknowns.
8. **Regulatory/MRM/CSRC source map** — authority, status, scope, and separation
   of public requirements from internal policy.
9. **Three required themes** — data privacy, interpretability/model governance,
   and outsourcing/third-party restrictions, supported at provision level.
10. **Implications and open applicability questions** — analysis for later
    feasibility work, not a legal/compliance conclusion.
11. **Findings, limitations, and remaining internship roadmap** — what Week 1–2
    establishes and what Weeks 3–8 will address.

## Evidence appendix

- Complete company database with source IDs.
- Company-by-company disclosure notes and maturity definitions.
- Regulatory source/provision matrix.
- Source-quality, terminology, and applicability notes.
- Unresolved questions and mentor/stakeholder inputs required.

## Out of scope for this checkpoint deck

- Ranked pilot recommendation.
- Model shortlist or benchmark result/design detail.
- Hardware architecture or sizing recommendation.
- TCO calculation.
- Final on-premise feasibility or compliance conclusion.
