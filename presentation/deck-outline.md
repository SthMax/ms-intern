# Phase 1 / Week 2 Presentation — 11 September 2026

Derived from Phase 1 of [the final plan](../PROJECT_PLAN.md) and
[the Week 2 execution plan](../weekly/week-02-plan.md).

The following is a proposed storyline; slide count and depth can be adjusted to
the available presentation time. Build the presentation from verified findings.
The industry assertions in the plan's overview need their own citations if used.

## Main storyline

1. **Project objective and Phase 1 questions** — onshore peer adoption and
   regulatory/MRM/CSRC implications within the confirmed eight-week project.
2. **Method and coverage** — company universe, asset-size definitions, source
   hierarchy, information cut-off, and actual verified company count.
3. **Company landscape** — initiatives by company and asset-size category, with
   comparable AUM metrics and maturity labels.
4. **Use cases and disclosed technology** — research, trading, risk, marketing,
   compliance; named partners, models, and stacks where evidenced.
5. **Selected cases and reported outcomes** — representative disclosures and
   limitations; distinguish company statements from vendor-reported outcomes.
6. **Regulatory and MRM source map** — issuer, instrument type, status, scope,
   and relationship to internal model risk management.
7. **Data privacy** — exact source provisions, interpretation, and applicability
   questions for the proposed deployment.
8. **Model interpretability and outsourcing** — provision-level evidence and
   model risk management considerations; distinguish source requirements from
   research recommendations.
9. **Phase 1 findings, gaps, and Phase 2 handoff** — verified conclusions,
   unresolved inputs, and technical questions to carry into Weeks 3–5.

If time allows, split the two themes on slide 8. The closing roadmap must use the
confirmed phases: Weeks 1–2 industry research; Weeks 3–5 technical feasibility;
Weeks 6–8 cost-benefit analysis and recommendations.

## Evidence appendix

- Complete company database, entity-count methodology, and asset-size definitions.
- Company disclosure/source notes, use-case taxonomy, and maturity definitions.
- Regulatory source/provision matrix and MRM source boundaries.
- Claim-to-source links and research cut-off.
- Unresolved questions, including the still-missing company-count requirement if
  it has not been supplied by presentation time.

## Slide checks

- Every material external factual claim has a source ID and a direct link.
- Relevant article/section/page is retrievable from its evidence note.
- A company counted once may have several initiative rows.
- Undisclosed partner, deployment, or outcome fields are labelled as such.
- Legal wording, applicability analysis, and proposed controls remain identifiable.
- The presentation reports Phase 1 work. Phase 2 and Phase 3 appear as the roadmap;
  model comparisons, GPU proof of concept, TCO results, and pilot prioritization
  will be developed in their assigned phases.
