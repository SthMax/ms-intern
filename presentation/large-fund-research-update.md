## Presentation discipline

Use named workflows, actual source wording and dated evidence. Distinguish
private/local/API/hybrid architectures and system components from contract
partners. Treat company-reported duration, coverage, edit adoption and
whole-platform cost gains as different metrics. No universal ROI estimate or
copy-and-deploy recommendation follows from these sources.
